How it works

Simple. Secure. Reliable.

Your traffic travels a private, encrypted path from your device to a dedicated residential IP — and only then out to the internet.

  1. Your Device

    Install the PointLink app on your laptop, desktop or mobile device and sign in.

  2. Encrypted Tunnel

    A secure VPN tunnel wraps every request so nobody in between can read it.

  3. Network Gateway

    Our gateway authenticates your device and routes it to your assigned IP.

  4. Residential IP

    Traffic exits from your dedicated residential IP in the location you chose.

  5. Internet

    Websites and work apps see one consistent, trusted residential connection.
