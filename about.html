We started IP PointLink because remote work broke the assumption that your connection and your location are the same thing. Our job is to give that consistency back — securely.

Our mission

Your Connection. Your Location. Your Privacy.

Modern security systems judge you by your IP address. Shared VPNs and rotating proxies make you look suspicious; hotel and café Wi-Fi make you look like someone new every day.

IP PointLink gives every customer a dedicated residential IP, reached through an encrypted point-to-point tunnel, so the services you depend on see one stable, trusted connection — wherever you actually are.
