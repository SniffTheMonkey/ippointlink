FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set up IP PointLink?
After signing up, download the app for your device from your dashboard, sign in, and press Connect. Your dedicated residential IP is assigned automatically within a few minutes.
Which devices and operating systems are supported?
macOS, Windows, iOS, Android and Linux, plus most OpenWrt-compatible routers. The number of simultaneous devices depends on your plan.
Can I choose where my residential IP is located?
Professional and Business plans include IP location selection. Starter plans are assigned the closest available location to the preference you choose at sign-up.
My connection dropped — what should I do?
The app reconnects automatically and the kill switch blocks traffic until the tunnel is restored. If it doesn't recover within a minute, quit and reopen the app, then check status.ippointlink.com.
Will PointLink slow down my internet?
Most customers see a small latency increase of 5–20 ms. Choosing an IP location near you keeps it minimal.
Is my residential IP ever shared or rotated?
No. Your IP is dedicated to your account and only changes if you request a new one.
How do I upgrade, downgrade or cancel?
Go to Dashboard → Billing. Changes take effect immediately for upgrades and at the next billing date for downgrades. You can cancel at any time.
Do you offer refunds?
Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime. Annual plans can be refunded on a prorated basis within the first 30 days.
Do you keep logs of my browsing?
No. We keep only what we need to operate and bill the service (account details, connection timestamps and bandwidth totals). We never log the sites you visit.
What is your acceptable use policy?
PointLink may only be used lawfully. You're responsible for complying with the terms and policies of your employer, clients and the services you access.
